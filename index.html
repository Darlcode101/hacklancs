About the event

HackLancs is a new student hackathon at Lancaster University. Turn up with an idea or without one, find a team, and spend the day building something you'd be proud to demo.

Software, hardware, games, tools, something strange: if you can show it by the evening, it counts. There are mentors when you get stuck, workshops if you want to learn something new, and food so you never have to leave your flow.

You don't need to have been to a hackathon before. Plenty of people ship their first ever project at one.

  • One dayFrom first idea to live demo
  • Free to attendFood and drink included
  • All skill levelsFirst-timers very welcome

FAQs

What is a hackathon?

A one-day event where you team up and build a project from scratch, then demo it to judges and everyone else at the end. Think of it as a very friendly deadline.

Who can attend?

HackLancs is for university students aged 18 or over. You don't have to study at Lancaster, and you don't have to study computing.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Attending is free, and food and drink are provided throughout the day.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Designers, makers and people with good ideas are all useful on a team, and there will be workshops and mentors to help you get started.

What if I don't have a team?

Most people don't. You apply as an individual, and there is a team formation session at the start for anyone who wants one.

How big can a team be?

Up to four people. Working solo is allowed too.

What should I bring?

A laptop and charger, your student ID, and any hardware you want to hack on. It is a long day, so a water bottle and a jumper are a good idea.

Where is it, and how do I get there?

On the Lancaster University campus at Bailrigg, a short bus ride south of the city centre and Lancaster railway station. The exact building will be confirmed with the dates.

What can I build, and who owns it?

Anything you like, as long as you start it at the event. Whatever you build belongs to you and your team.

I have another question.

Email hello@hacklancs.com and we'll get back to you.
