House rules

Code of conduct

HackLancs should be a friendly, safe and welcoming day for everyone. This code applies to everyone involved: attendees, mentors, judges, sponsors, volunteers and organisers, at the venue and in our online spaces, including the Discord.

The short version

Be kind, be respectful, and help make the day good for everyone else. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

What we expect

  • Treat everyone with respect, whatever their background, experience or skill level.
  • Be welcoming to first-timers. Everyone started somewhere.
  • Respect the venue, the equipment and the people who look after them.
  • Follow any instructions from organisers and venue staff, especially about safety.
  • Start your project at the event, and only use code, assets and data you have the right to use.

What isn't acceptable

  • Offensive comments or behaviour relating to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age or any other characteristic.
  • Intimidation, stalking, unwanted photography or recording, or unwelcome physical contact or sexual attention.
  • Sexual or violent language or imagery in projects, presentations, the Discord or anywhere else at the event.
  • Sustained disruption of talks, workshops or other people's work.
  • Hacking, attacking or tampering with anyone else's devices, accounts or networks, including the venue's.

If something goes wrong

If you experience or see something that breaks this code, or anything else makes you uncomfortable, talk to any organiser on the day, or email hello@hacklancs.com. We'll take every report seriously and handle it as confidentially as we can.

If anyone is in immediate danger, call 999, then let an organiser or Lancaster University Security know.

What happens next

Organisers may take any action they think is appropriate. That could be a warning, removal from the event, disqualification from prizes, or reporting the matter to the university or the police.

This code is adapted from common practice across student hackathons. Questions about it? Email hello@hacklancs.com.
